Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

Do you take custom cake orders?

Yes! We specialize in custom cakes for all occasions. Please contact us at least 72 hours in advance for custom orders to ensure we can create your perfect cake.

Do you offer gluten-free or vegan options?

We have a selection of gluten-free and vegan treats available daily. For specific dietary requirements, we recommend calling ahead to check availability or placing a custom order.

Can I place an order for pickup?

Absolutely! You can place orders through our website, by phone, or in person. We recommend ordering at least 24 hours in advance for larger items.

Do you offer catering for events?

Yes, we provide catering services for events of all sizes. Contact us with your event details, and we'll create a customized package for you.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, credit/debit cards, and mobile payments. We also offer contactless payment options for your convenience.

Do you have parking available?

Yes, we have dedicated customer parking available right outside our bakery for your convenience.
